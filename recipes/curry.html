Japanese Curry Rice

Japanese curry rice is a comforting, mildly spiced dish featuring tender meat and vegetables simmered in a rich, savory curry sauce and served over fluffy steamed rice. Simple to prepare and deeply satisfying, it's a beloved home-cooked meal that's perfect for busy weeknights or cozy family dinners.

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb cubed beef
  • 2 medium onions
  • 2 medium potatoes
  • 2 medium carrots
  • 2 tablespoons neutral cooking oil
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 package of Japanese curry roux cubes
  • 4 cups cooked Japanese short-grain rice

Steps:

  1. Prepare the ingredients. Cut the meat into bite-sized pieces, dice the onions, and peel and chop carrots and potatoes into roughly equal-sized chunks.
  2. Cook the meat. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the meat and cook until lightly browned, about 3-5 minutes.
  3. Cook the vegetables. Add the onions and cook for 5-8 minutes until softened and lightly golden. Stir in the carrots and potatoes for about 2 mintues.
  4. Simmer. Pour in the water. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low. Cover and let simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender.
  5. Add the curry roux. Turn off the heat. Break the curry roux into pieces and stir until fully dissolved. Return the pot to low heat and simmer for another 5-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the curry thickens.
  6. Serve with cooked rice. Enjoy!
